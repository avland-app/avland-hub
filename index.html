The problem

AV estimates should not take all night.

QuickBooks is great for accounting and invoices, but it was not designed around AV quotes. Excel and Google Sheets work at first, but they get messy when you need revisions, rooms, labor, rentals, PDFs, and pull sheets.

  • → You copy an old spreadsheet and hope the formulas still work.
  • → Client names, gear, labor, tax, and discounts live in too many places.
  • → Pull sheets have to be rebuilt manually after the quote is approved.
  • → Big rental software can be too expensive or too complicated for small AV teams.
  • → A simple quote revision can turn into a new file, new PDF, and new headache.
Features

Built around the way AV quotes actually work

1

Room-based Quote Builder

Organize line items by room or area. Add Audio, Video, Lighting, Labor, Delivery, and support items in a clean quote layout.

2

Saved Item Library

Save your gear, labor, and service rates once, then pull them into new quotes fast.

3

Clean PDF Proposals

Generate client-ready PDFs with your branding, quote totals, deposits, tax, and optional line item visibility.

4

Pull Sheets

Turn rental items into an internal pull sheet for warehouse prep, crew notes, load-in, and sub-rental tracking.

5

Client Database

Save client details and reuse them in future quotes instead of digging through old emails and spreadsheets.

6

Labor, OT & DT

Track labor day rates, overtime, and double time in a way that makes sense for AV production.
