How a job runs through GaffDesk

From the first email to getting paid, one record follows the job. Nothing gets retyped between the quote, the invoice and the warehouse.

CueWhat happensIn GaffDesk
Cue1
The request comes in

Start a quote from a saved client, a template or a past job you can duplicate.

Cue2
You build the quote

Pick gear and labor from your own item library at your own rates. Kits add their parts automatically. Crew lines work out overtime and double time from the hours you enter, and you can override them.

Cue3
You check the gear

See whether owned gear is free on those dates before you promise it. Anything you don't own goes on the quote as a sub-rental with its source.

Cue4
You send it

Email a PDF with your logo and terms straight from GaffDesk. Changes become Rev B, Rev C, with the history kept.

Cue5
The client approves

Convert the approved quote into an invoice in one step, with every line carried over.

Cue6
The warehouse preps

Print a pull sheet with load-in details and the source of every item, so prep matches what was sold.

Cue7
You get paid

Send the invoice to QuickBooks as a draft with the PDF attached, if you use the QuickBooks add-on.

Questions

Do I need a credit card to start the trial?

No. The 7-day trial gives you full Pro access without a card. At the end of the trial, choose a plan to keep using GaffDesk.

Is GaffDesk accounting software?

No. GaffDesk handles quotes, invoices, crew, gear and pull sheets. Keep using QuickBooks or your accountant for bookkeeping. With the QuickBooks add-on, approved invoices go to QuickBooks Online as drafts.

Can I use my own gear list and prices?

Yes. Your item library holds your own gear, labor and services with your rates, categories, tax settings and terms. You can import it from a spreadsheet.

How does crew overtime work?

Enter the hours per day on a labor line and GaffDesk splits them into regular, overtime and double time using your company's labor rules. You can override the split on any line.

What's the difference between Event and Install?

Event is for shows and rentals, with rental days, crew calls and pull sheets. Install is for installation projects, organized by room or area with material lists. Pro and Team can include both.

Can my team use it?

Yes. The Team plan includes up to 3 people sharing clients, the item library and quotes, with admin controls over who can do what.

Who can see my clients and prices?

Only you and the team members you add. Your clients, prices and quotes stay in your account.
