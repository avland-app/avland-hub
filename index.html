The problem

Excel was never built for AV quotes.

AV companies are running proposals through spreadsheets that break, get emailed back and forth, and don't know the difference between a labor day rate and an OT hour. You end up copying formulas, reformatting PDFs, and chasing version history.

  • Formatting a quote PDF takes longer than writing the quote itself
  • No built-in revision history — "final_v3_REAL.xlsx" is not a workflow
  • Pull sheets have to be rebuilt manually from the quote every time
  • Labor OT and DT calculations done by hand, prone to errors
  • Client history scattered across emails, folders, and spreadsheets
Features

Everything an AV company actually needs

Room-based Quote Builder

Organize line items by room or area — General Session, Breakout, Ballroom. Categories auto-sort Audio, Video, Lighting, Labor, and more.

Saved Item Library

Build a catalog of your gear and labor rates. Select from the library while quoting — rates auto-fill. Import from Excel, export any time.

Clean PDF Proposals

Print-ready proposals with your logo, company info, and pricing. Control which prices show — hide line items, show department totals only.

Pull Sheet Generator

Mark items as rental and generate a pull sheet for warehouse prep — with sub-rental tracking, load-in times, and checkbox columns for your crew.

Client Database

Save client info and pull it into quotes with one click. See the full quote history for any client — every revision, every status, every total.

Labor OT & DT Calculations

Set base hours per day, add OT and DT hours — Desk calculates the blended total automatically at 1.5× and 2× the hourly rate.

How it works

From blank to proposal in minutes

1

Build your library

Add your gear, labor, and services with day rates. Import from Excel if you already have a list. Takes 10 minutes once.

2

Create a quote

Add rooms, select items from your library, enter quantities. Discounts, tax, deposits, and OT are built in.

3

Print or share

Print to PDF directly from the browser — your logo, your branding, clean layout. Generate the pull sheet for your crew in one click.
